# Dengue Care Guideline (synthetic test fixture)

## 1. Purpose
This synthetic document exists only to test the platform. It is not medical guidance.

## 2. Warning Signs
- Severe abdominal pain or persistent vomiting are warning signs of severe dengue.
- Bleeding from the gums or nose is a warning sign that needs urgent review.
- Restlessness or lethargy is a warning sign in children.

## 3. Management

### 3.1 Fever Control
Paracetamol is used to control fever in dengue. Aspirin and ibuprofen are avoided because they increase the risk of bleeding.

### 3.2 Monitoring
Patients with warning signs are reviewed by a clinician every 2 hours. Haematocrit is checked before and after each fluid bolus.

## 4. Discharge
Patients can be discharged when they have had no fever for 48 hours without paracetamol and their platelet count is rising.
